Linkd · relationship memory for professionals

The right introduction is usually someone you've already met.

You meet people constantly, and the details fade within weeks. Linkd turns your conversations into a memory you can actually use, so the people who matter don't slip away.

Your network is bigger than your memory.

The details decay fast. A week after a good conversation, you've lost the specifics. What they're building, what they needed, why it mattered that you two met.

So when the moment finally arrives to make the connection, the investor for a friend's round, the hire for a former colleague, the person who's been where you're headed, you can't remember who fits. The value was always there. It just wasn't in reach.

A relationship has three moments. Linkd is there for each.

  1. 01

    Before you meet

    See who in your network is worth reaching out to before you ever walk into the room.

  2. 02

    When you meet

    Record a quick voice note after a conversation, and Linkd turns it into a clean, structured profile.

  3. 03

    After you meet

    Get a nudge, with the context, to follow up while it still matters.
